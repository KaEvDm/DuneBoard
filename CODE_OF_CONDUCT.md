# Code of Conduct

This project should stay practical, direct, and respectful.

## Expected Behavior

- Discuss ideas and code on their merits.
- Be clear when giving feedback.
- Assume good faith unless behavior proves otherwise.
- Keep disagreements focused on the work.
- Help keep issues and pull requests actionable.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Dismissive comments about identity, background, or experience.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove comments and issues that make the
project harder to work on. Repeated or severe violations may lead to a ban from
project spaces.
